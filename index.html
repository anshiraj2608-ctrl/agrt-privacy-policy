# AGRT – Privacy Policy **Last Updated:** November 8, 2026 ## 1. Introduction Automatic Group RT (AGRT) is a Chrome extension designed to help authorized users manage retweet workflows on supported X (formerly Twitter) pages. We respect user privacy and handle information only as necessary to provide the extension's functionality, license verification and account management. ## 2. Information We Handle Depending on the features used, AGRT may handle: - **License information:** License keys and license status. - **Device information:** Device public key and browser name. - **Account information:** X username/handle. - **Authentication information:** Session tokens used to maintain authorized access. - **Extension information:** Browser version and extension version. - **Website content:** Limited X chat-cache information used by supported link-related features. ## 3. How We Use Information Information is used to: - Verify license validity. - Register and manage authorized devices. - Enforce account and device limits. - Maintain authenticated extension sessions. - Support retweet workflows and extension functionality. - Provide license approval, expiry and revocation controls. ## 4. Information Transmission License-related information is transmitted to the AGRT Google Apps Script licensing service. The extension may also communicate with X-related services, including t.co, for supported link functionality. We do not intentionally transmit direct messages, group names, message text or numeric X user IDs to the AGRT licensing server, according to the current client-side implementation. ## 5. Local Data Processing and Storage Some extension settings, session information and supported X chat-cache data are processed or stored locally on the user's device. The extension may access limited chat-cache information for link-related functionality. It does not intentionally send the full chat database to the AGRT licensing server. ## 6. Data Sharing and Selling We do not sell users' personal information. Information is not shared with third parties for advertising or unrelated purposes. Data may be transmitted to the services required for AGRT functionality, including Google Apps Script and X-related services. ## 7. Data Security AGRT uses license verification, device registration and session controls to protect access. Users should keep their license keys and authentication credentials confidential. ## 8. Data Retention and Deletion License and account records may be retained for licensing, security and administrative purposes. Users may contact the AGRT administrator to request assistance with account or data deletion, subject to applicable operational and legal requirements. ## 9. Children's Privacy AGRT is not designed specifically for children and should not be used by individuals who are not permitted to use the supported services. ## 10. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when AGRT functionality or data-handling practices change. ## 11.
